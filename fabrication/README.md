# Fabrication package

`V9958-Tester-rev0.2-gerbers.zip` contains the two copper layers, solder masks, front/back silkscreens, board outline, KiCad Gerber job file, and separate plated/non-plated Excellon drill files.

Nominal order parameters:

- 2 layers
- 1.6 mm FR-4
- 1 oz copper
- Green solder mask, white silkscreen
- 231 mm x 140 mm outline
- Minimum released trace/space: 0.25 mm / 0.20 mm
- Smallest plated drill: 0.35 mm (vias)
- V9958 socket drill: 0.90 mm
- Four 3.20 mm NPTH mounting holes

This is revision 0.2, an electrically checked but physically unbuilt prototype. Inspect the Gerber preview and confirm the chosen V9958 socket/ZIF and DIN-8 connector dimensions before ordering.
